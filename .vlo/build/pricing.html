Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VLO pricing.

Is VLO really free?

Yes. The core VLO framework is intended to remain free and open source. The Pro and Enterprise plans are designed for teams and additional services.

Can I use VLO commercially?

Yes. VLO can be used to build personal, commercial, and open-source projects.

Do I need a credit card?

No. You can start using the free tier without entering payment information.

When will Pro launch?

Pro is planned for a future release. Join the waitlist to stay informed when availability is announced.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Start with the free tier and move to a paid plan when your project or team needs additional capabilities.

Where can I learn VLO?

Visit the documentation to learn how to install VLO, create components, and build your first project.
