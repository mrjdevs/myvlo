01 — WHAT IS VLO?

A better way to build interfaces.

VLO is built around a simple idea: interfaces should be easy to create, reuse and maintain.

Instead of repeatedly writing the same UI, developers can create reusable components and customize them through props.

The result is cleaner code, consistent design and faster development.
